Your photo, specifically

Virtual try-on works by sending your photo together with a garment image to a third-party AI model. This means:

  • Your photo leaves our servers and is processed by Replicate, Inc., which runs the try-on model.
  • Photos of clothing are also sent to Anthropic for automatic tagging, and to a background-removal service for cutouts.
  • These providers process the images to return a result. We do not grant them permission to use your images to train models.
  • Your content is visible only to you. Cher has no public feed and no way to browse other people's wardrobes or posts.

If you are not comfortable with this, do not add a photo of yourself — every other feature works without one.

How long we keep it

Your content is kept until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account we remove your garments, outfits, posts, uploaded photos, credit balance and transaction history, and the account itself. Deletion is immediate and permanent — we do not keep backups you can be restored from.

Images sent to AI providers for processing are retained according to those providers' own policies, which we do not control.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → menu → Delete account. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm.

If you have already uninstalled the app, see the account deletion page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Deleting your account satisfies erasure immediately; for anything else, email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Cher is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on Supabase with row-level security, so one account cannot read another's data. No system is perfectly secure; if we discover a breach affecting your data we will notify you.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
