Delete your Cher account

Cher — Virtual Wardrobe

If you still have the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately:

  1. Open Cher and go to Profile.
  2. Open the menu and choose Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

If you have uninstalled the app

Email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We verify the request against that address, so please send it from the same one.

We will confirm once it is done, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Every clothing item, outfit, canvas and post
  • Every photo you uploaded, including your try-on photo
  • Your try-on credit balance and transaction history
Deletion is permanent. There are no backups to restore from, and any unused try-on credits are lost and are not refundable.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous, aggregated error logs may persist for a short period; they contain no photos and no account identifiers.

Purchase records held by Google Play are controlled by Google, not by us. To ask about those, contact Google Play support.
